## A few strategic changes I'd make

Compared to Brittany Chiang's portfolio, I'd also:

* Replace **"Experience"** with **"Process"** or **"Services."** Business owners care more about how you'll work with them than where you've worked.
* Move **"Featured Projects"** much higher on the page. Your work should sell itself early.
* Add measurable outcomes where possible (e.g., "Redesigned a real estate website to improve clarity and showcase properties more effectively") rather than focusing on technologies.
* Keep the technology stack concise and secondary. Mention it to demonstrate capability, but let the business value lead the narrative.
* Use language centered on the client's goals—credibility, trust, inquiries, and conversions—instead of developer-centric terms like frameworks and tooling.

The overall tone should make visitors think: **"This person understands businesses, not just websites."** That positioning aligns well with the clients you're targeting, such as consultancies, real estate firms, travel agencies, and other service businesses.
